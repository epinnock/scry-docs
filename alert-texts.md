# Alert texts and calls

**Last updated:** September 27, 2026

Scry's monitoring service ("Scry pager") sends operational alerts about Scry's own services to the phone number its account owner has configured. It is not a marketing program. No one other than the account owner receives these messages.

## What you receive

Text messages and phone calls from **+1 844-456-5700** when a Scry service check fails or recovers; for severe incidents, a phone call. Frequency varies with incidents, typically a few messages a month. Message and data rates may apply.

## How the pager number is set (consent)

The account owner enters their own mobile number as the pager destination in the monitoring service's configuration (the `PAGER_TO` setting) and confirms it by answering a test call. By setting the number, the account owner agrees to receive these alert texts and calls at that number. Only the account owner can set, change or remove the number.

## Opt out and help

Reply **STOP** to any text to stop receiving texts; reply **HELP** for help. Removing the number from the configuration also stops all texts and calls. Questions: [support@scrymore.com](mailto:support@scrymore.com).

## Sample messages

- "Scry alert: production P0. Diff service has been down for 12 minutes. We are calling you now. Reply STOP to opt out."
- "Scry alert: production main-branch check recovered after 41 minutes. No action needed. Reply STOP to opt out."

## Privacy

The phone number is stored only in the monitoring service's configuration, is used only for these alerts, and is never shared or sold. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
