# Account settings

**Account** in the dashboard holds settings that belong to you rather than to a workspace or a project..

## Help

**Account ▸ Help** (`/account/help`) is where you replay the dashboard tours.

- **Replay tours** lists each tour, **Welcome** and **Your first project**, with whether you finished or skipped it. Choose **Replay** to clear it and start it again where it begins: the Projects page for Welcome, your most recent project for Your first project.
- If you have no project yet, Replay for Your first project is disabled until you create one.

![Account Help with a Replay button for each tour](/images/coachmarks/help-done-skipped-storybook.png)

Tours are described in [Quick Start](/guide/quick-start#the-dashboard-tour). They show once, only on screens 900 pixels wide or more.
