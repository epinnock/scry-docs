You own what you upload: Storybooks, screenshots, Figma renders, notes and so on. You let us store, process and show it only as needed to run Scry for you and the people you share it with. **We don't train AI models on your content.** Only upload things you have the right to upload.

**Public projects can be seen by anyone**, including through search and the MCP server. Only make a project public if you're allowed to publish it.
